Training log and progress.
Train has a new button: Add from photos. Pick up to eight pictures of your workouts (the board, the
class screen, your tracking app). Luma reads them, splits them into separate workouts, sorts each by
type and lists every exercise with its sets. A weight shown in the picture is filled in for you. Where
none is shown, the exercise has an empty box asking for the weight you used; one tap offers last
time's. You can give each set its own weight, type in an exercise by hand, or enter a whole workout
with no photo. Reading photos uses your coach key.
Train also has a new Progress view. A body figure, front and back, shows each muscle's strength level,
how it is moving, and how much work it got this week; tap a muscle for the numbers. Below it: strong
and weak points, each lift's estimated max over time, sets by muscle, your food set against training
days, how often you took each supplement, and what your habits do to the next morning.
Strength levels are Luma's own scale and the maxes are estimates from your logged sets. A muscle with
no loaded lift behind it shows as not measured, not as weak.

Two experiments: counting from wrist motion, and a trace from the clasp.
Both are off until you switch them on, on the Strap screen. Neither has been tried on a strap yet.
Counting from motion: with "Record motion with sessions" and "Count from motion" both on, Luma
counts from the movement of your wrist after a session: steps a minute in a run, strokes on the
SkiErg and rower, reps at a station. A session with parts (a simulation, intervals, a led workout)
is counted part by part. A session with none, such as a class recorded as one piece, is split into
runs, work and rest. The counts show on the session's screen and under each recording on the Strap
screen, and change nothing in the session itself.
It is an estimate, checked so far only against made-up movement. Enter your own number beside a
count and Luma shows how far off it has been. Walking about between sets is counted with them.
Trace from the clasp (WHOOP MG only): holding the clasp with your other hand for about 40 seconds
gives a trace of your heartbeat. Luma counts its beats for a heart rate, and shows how much the time
between beats varied. It is a trial: the strap may not answer on this software, and the screen says
what the strap answered either way.
The trace is a recording, not a check of your heart. Luma does not look for rhythm problems and
shows nothing the strap's own software concluded. See a doctor about any worry.

Accuracy update.
Heart rate is now live on every screen and on the Lock Screen card: the strap streams to the phone all
the time, not only while the Heart screen is open. This uses more strap battery; it can be switched off
under Settings, Recording. Keep Luma open in the background overnight: swiping it away stops the recording.
Luma no longer shows a figure it did not measure. When part of a night never reached the phone it says
so, with the times, marks that night's numbers, and leaves the night out of sleep debt and averages.
A missing recovery score now comes with its real reason, for example how many more recorded nights it needs.
New under Settings: Data check shows, hour by hour, where heart rate reached the phone, and sends a report.

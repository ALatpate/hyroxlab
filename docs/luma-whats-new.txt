The strap does more, the numbers go deeper, the coach and Glimm are smarter.
Wake alarm, on the Strap screen: one wake time, the days it goes off on, and a plain reading of what
is set to happen: the notification on the phone, and whether the strap has been told. On a WHOOP 5.0
or MG the strap's own alarm is a trial behind its own switch. Keep another alarm for anything you
cannot miss.
Breathing, on the Strap screen: paced breathing with a buzz in and out, heart rate and beat-to-beat
variation as you go.
Buzzes by heart rate (off until you switch them on): above a heart-rate zone in a steady session,
and "go again" in the rests of an interval run when your heart rate has come down.
Led workouts: Go, Lead me through a workout. Type it or photograph it, check what Luma read, and it
leads you through with a buzz and a spoken cue at every change.
Heart rate for gym equipment (experimental, off until switched on): the phone stands in as a
heart-rate sensor for a treadmill, a bike or another app, while Luma is on screen or recording.
Motion recording (experimental, off until switched on): records the strap's raw movement beside a
session, to work out later how to count reps and strokes. Nothing is read from it yet.
Trends: HRV against your own normal band, an early word when resting heart rate creeps up over
several nights, sleep regularity, and your week's load against your own usual week.
Running: heart rate at your easy pace run by run, critical speed from your fastest efforts, and for
HYROX sessions where the time went and how heart rate held late on. A run's route exports as GPX.
Coach: does its sums with a calculator, plans from more of your numbers, and can search the web
when you switch that on (it keeps you out of the search words and lists what it found).
Glimm learns by itself: each day it reads your last two weeks, writes down what it has noticed,
crosses out what no longer holds and says when it has learned something. Take a note out and it is
not written again. It also has fresh lines each day, small jobs it does on a tap, and you can talk
to it aloud (speech is turned into words on the phone).
New builds: Luma can look once a day for a newer build and say so on Today, if you switch it on.
Not tried on a strap yet: the buzzes, the alarm, sharing heart rate and motion recording.

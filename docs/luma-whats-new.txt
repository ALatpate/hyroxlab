Accuracy update.
Luma now asks the strap to send heart rate all the time, not only while the Heart screen is open, so
every screen and the Lock Screen card can show it live and whole nights can be recorded. This uses more
strap battery; switch it off under Settings, Recording. Keep Luma open in the background overnight:
swiping it away stops the recording.
Luma no longer shows a figure it did not measure. When part of a night, a session or the day never
reached the phone it says so, with the times, marks the numbers, and leaves that night out of sleep debt
and averages. A missing recovery score comes with its real reason, such as how many recorded nights it
still needs.
New under Settings: Data check shows, hour by hour, where heart rate reached the phone, and sends a report.

Luma Doctor, and a new Glimm.
Luma Doctor (Recovery, Weekly check): every Sunday it checks the week against your own usual range:
resting heart rate, HRV, breathing, skin temperature, blood oxygen, sleep, bedtime, recovery and
training load. It keeps a log of every check. With your key connected and health sharing on, the
strongest model your key reaches writes the read, whatever the coach is set to. It is not a doctor:
it says what moved and when something is worth showing to one.
Glimm is new: a face that shows how it feels, a leaf, freckles, and clothes. 2,944 things to wear;
it dresses for the day (training, a slow evening, bed) and changes every day. Like or pass on an
outfit and it learns your taste. Open the wardrobe to dress it yourself.
It moves: it breathes, blinks, looks about, waves, yawns, stretches and checks its trainers. Tap the
leaf, the head, a cheek, the belly, a hand or the feet and it answers.
Talk with it: with your key connected, it answers in its own voice, changes clothes when asked, and
can offer to remember something. Nothing is remembered without your yes.
Glimm is on the Lock Screen heart-rate card, and in its own widget, dressed the same.

A companion, a new look, and twelve new reads of your data.
Glim: a small companion on Today and as a new Lock Screen and Home Screen widget (add "Companion").
It is charged, steady or drained with your recovery, asleep in your usual night, thirsty when water
is behind, in session when you train, and it celebrates a new best. Its build follows your own
strength levels, part by part. Tap it for a wave. Open it to see why it looks the way it does.
New type: tall, narrow titles and figures, and text that follows your iPhone's text size.
Motion: a tile opens into its screen, muscles fill in, rows shimmer while photos are read, a banner
for a new best, ticks that draw themselves, and a heart that beats at your real rate (and is still
when there is no live reading).
New analysis: supplement trials (blocks on and off, a fair answer); how much and how late (caffeine,
alcohol, last meal, bedtime against the next morning, with your own caffeine cut-off); food against
the work, day by day; a weight log with what you really burn; a kept weekly review; what goes with
your stronger weeks; one model of what moves your recovery, with tomorrow's likely score; each
lift's history and stalled lifts; your own sweat rate; an offer to ease off after two low mornings.
Log without opening Luma: a water button on the Fuel widget, and "Log water in Luma" for Siri.
Reminders, all off until you switch them on: Settings, Reminders.

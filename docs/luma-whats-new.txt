Your records are safe, and typing works.
Fixed: Luma could be started by the strap while the phone was locked, find it could not open your
records, take them for empty, ask for your details again and save over what you had logged. It now
waits until it can read them and never saves over what it has not read.
New: a copy of everything you log is kept for each of the last 14 days. Settings, Your data, Copies
of your records: go back to a day if anything goes missing, and undo it if you change your mind.
Typing: the box you are typing in stays above the keyboard, on the coach and in every form. Sending
a message puts the keyboard away and the box goes back to one line.
SkiErg, RowErg, BikeErg, runs and carries: a box for distance, time or calories when you check a
workout read from a photo, when you add one by hand, and in a live session. No weight is asked for.
The minus and plus buttons take the whole button, keep going while held, and a figure can be typed
by tapping it (year, height, body mass).
Fixed: removing a food item, a set or an exercise could stop the app.
Data check now shows how your records were read and what iOS reports when the app stops.

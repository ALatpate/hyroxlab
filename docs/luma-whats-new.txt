Glimm dresses itself.
The wardrobe and the outfit buttons are gone. Nobody dresses Glimm and nothing rates its clothes: it
picks for what the day is (training, a slow evening, bed) and for how the day feels, brighter on a
charged day and softer on a drained one.
Its taste is its own. It has favourites it comes back to, it goes through phases with a colour, and
the top it trained in on the day of a new best becomes its lucky one.
It says why it picked what it has on, in its own words: open Glimm, under "What it picked".
When the day moves on while you are looking, it turns into its new clothes by itself.
Talk with it: suggest an outfit or a colour and it decides for itself. Say something kind about its
clothes and it grows fond of them. Its replies read more like a friend's and less like an assistant's.
If you skipped build 18: Luma Doctor is in Recovery, Weekly check, and Glimm has its new look.
